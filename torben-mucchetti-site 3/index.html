Meet Torben

Torben Mucchetti is a country singer-songwriter and guitarist bringing a rock edge to heartfelt, guitar-driven music.

Whether he’s playing solo with an acoustic guitar or stepping onstage with a full band, his sound is built around original songs, live energy, and a love for country and rock.

From singing with a guitar as a kid to performing today, the goal is still the same: make music that feels honest and connects with people.
